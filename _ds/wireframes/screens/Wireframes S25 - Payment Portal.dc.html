1Sequence 25 — Payment Portal (proposed, no repo source — confirmed by full-repo search, no payment/billing code exists anywhere). Citizen-facing, unauthenticated, reuses Sequences 22/23/24's public mobile-browser frame. Two payable instruments: an issued infringement (PIN) and an approved permit's fee. A shared declined-payment state closes both flows.
Form factors: 25a, 25c and 25f redrawn at tablet/laptop in Turn 2 below — this is a public web portal (not the EnforcementMAPS native app), so its own responsive coverage sits in this file rather than the "EnforcementMAPS Tablet & Laptop Coverage" cross-cutting file; 25b, 25d, 25e, 25g and 25h are layout-identical across form factors.
