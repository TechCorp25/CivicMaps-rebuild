Optimised Foot‑Patrol Route for SPENCER and FLAGSTAFF Zones

Starting point: Bourke Street × William Street (south‑east corner of the SPENCER zone).
Patrol objective: cover every boundary street that is marked as solid/included and every internal street/laneway in the SPENCER and FLAGSTAFF zones.
Key parking restrictions (from public sources):

Clearways & “no‑stopping” times.
– City of Melbourne’s parking‑improvement works indicate that clearways on some CBD streets have been simplified. A clearway is a zone where stopping is prohibited at times shown on the sign. On Lonsdale Street (between Spencer & Spring streets) the AM clearway now operates 7 – 10 AM and the PM clearway 4 – 7 PM on weekdays; outside those times parking restrictions revert to the signed time limits.
– Spencer Street’s former northbound clearway (Flinders → Collins) has been removed, but “no‑stopping” sections remain on Flinders Street to manage peak‑period traffic.
– Queen Street (Lonsdale → Flinders) has a PM clearway from 4 PM to 7 PM on weekdays (important when approaching the SPENCER zone from the east).
Loading zones. Only eligible vehicles (commercial vehicles, taxis, vehicles with commercial signage or G‑class registration) may use loading zones; general vehicles may not stop there. Many loading zones convert to ordinary parking after 4 PM—officers should enforce restrictions only during the posted hours. The City of Melbourne provides an interactive map of loading‑zone locations. In the West Melbourne/Flagstaff area, the map shows numerous loading‑zone bays (red markers) on William Street (between Dudley and Walsh streets), Rosslyn Street (near Spencer Street) and a few along King Street. There is also a car‑share bay (purple marker) on Rosslyn Street, and accessible parking bays (blue markers) on King Street and side streets.
Permit/parking bays. The City’s kerbside‑management maps designate various time‑limited parking bays (1P, 2P, 3P, 4P) and “resident permit excepted” bays. Within the FLAGSTAFF zone the map shows:
– Loading zone bays: mainly on William Street and one on Rosslyn Street.
– Car‑share bay: on Rosslyn Street near Capel Street.
– Accessible bays: on King Street near the intersection with Rosslyn Street and near Dudley Street (blue markers).
– Pick‑up/drop‑off bay: one is marked near the King‑Rosslyn intersection. These should be checked for compliance during patrol.
1. Patrol plan – SPENCER Zone

The SPENCER zone lies between Spencer Street (west) and William Street (east) and from Lonsdale Street (north – broken/excluded boundary) to Collins Street (south – broken/excluded boundary). Internal streets include Little Bourke Street, Bourke Street, Little Collins Street (east–west) and King Street (north–south). The goal is to cover each included boundary and internal street once, minimising back‑tracking.

Route steps (SPENCER zone)
Step	Description & important checks	Rationale
1. William St (Bourke → Lonsdale)	From the starting point at William × Bourke, walk north along William Street to Lonsdale Street. Inspect the loading‑zone bays and clearway‑signed segments. 2. Lonsdale St (William → King)	Cross Lonsdale Street heading west to King Street. Lonsdale is a broken boundary (not patrolled) but is used to reposition. 3. King St (Lonsdale → Little Bourke)	Turn south on King Street. King Street is an internal patrol street; check for accessible bays and loading zones (map shows accessible bays on King between Lonsdale and Little Lonsdale). Continue to Little Bourke Street.	Starts patrolling the north part of the zone.
4. Little Bourke St loop	At Little Bourke × King, turn west along Little Bourke Street to Spencer Street. This street contains several loading‑zone bays (particularly near the Spencer end). 6. Bourke St loop	From King × Bourke, go west along Bourke Street to Spencer Street, checking for loading zones and time‑limited bays. Reverse direction and walk east to William Street, patrolling the entire street. Return to King Street.	Covers the second east–west internal street.

9. King St (Little Collins → Collins)	Walk south on King Street to Collins Street (southern end of the SPENCER zone). This finishes the north–south patrol.	Reaches the southern boundary.

11. Spencer St (Collins → Lonsdale)	Walk north along Spencer Street from Collins to Lonsdale, patrolling the western boundary of the zone. Spencer Street carries heavy traffic and has a former clearway (northbound clearway removed); check that vehicles respect bus‑lane/no‑stopping signs.	Completes boundary patrol.
12. Lonsdale St (Spencer → William)	Finally, use Lonsdale Street to return east to William Street, and walk south back to the starting point at Bourke × William, closing the loop.	Ends SPENCER patrol.

Estimated distance: ~2.3 km (allows for cross‑street loops).
High‑priority enforcement points: loading‑zone bays on Little Bourke, Bourke and Little Collins Streets; accessible bays on King Street; clearway times on Lonsdale Street.

2. Patrol plan – FLAGSTAFF Zone

The FLAGSTAFF zone lies north of the SPENCER zone, between Spencer Street (west) and William Street (east) and from Jeffcott/Dudley Street (north – solid boundary) south to Lonsdale Street (broken/excluded boundary). Internal east–west streets include Franklin Street, A’Beckett Street, La Trobe Street, Little Lonsdale Street, Rosslyn Street and Walsh Street. Internal north–south streets include King Street, Chetwynd Street and Capel Street. Below is a serpentine route that covers each street with minimal duplication.

William Street has many loading‑zone bays (red markers on the West Melbourne map) between Dudley and Walsh streets, so pay close attention to commercial‑vehicle compliance.	Covers the eastern boundary of FLAGSTAFF.

Covers internal spur and checks car‑share bay.

Turn north along Capel Street to Dudley/Jeffcott Street, patrolling this internal north‑south street. Then walk south along King Street all the way to Lonsdale Street, ensuring the full length between Jeffcott and Lonsdale is patrolled.	Completes patrol of major north–south street.
17. Exit the zone	From King × Lonsdale, use Lonsdale Street to return to William Street and back to the SPENCER starting point or continue east/west depending on the next assignment.	Ends FLAGSTAFF patrol.

Estimated distance: ~3 km for the main serpentine route plus spurs and loops.

3. Summary of parking‑restriction insights
Lonsdale Street clearways. Clearway times on Lonsdale Street have been simplified to 7–10 AM westbound and 4–7 PM eastbound Monday–Friday; outside these hours, normal parking rules apply. Officers should enforce no‑stopping during clearway periods but need not patrol Lonsdale Street outside those times (broken boundary).
Spencer Street clearway removal and no‑stopping areas. The northbound clearway on Spencer Street (Flinders → Collins) has been removed, but “no‑stopping” sections remain on Flinders Street. In the FLAGSTAFF zone, Spencer Street serves as a boundary; officers should watch for illegally stopped vehicles in bus lanes or no‑stopping sections.
Loading zones and permit bays. The City of Melbourne’s loading‑zone guidelines state that only specific vehicles (G‑class vehicles, commercial passenger vehicles and vehicles with permanent commercial signage) may use loading zones. In the West Melbourne/Flagstaff area, the kerbside‑management map shows numerous loading‑zone bays on William Street, a loading‑zone bay on Rosslyn Street, and several along King Street; these should be priority enforcement points. Many of these loading zones convert to general parking after 4 PM.
Car‑share and accessible bays. A car‑share bay is located on Rosslyn Street near Capel Street (purple marker on the map). Accessible parking bays are located on King Street near Rosslyn Street and near Dudley Street. These bays should be checked for permit compliance and kept clear for eligible users.
Pick‑up/drop‑off bays. The kerbside‑management map indicates a pick‑up/drop‑off bay near the King Street × Rosslyn Street intersection. Vehicles may stop briefly in these bays to load or unload passengers but should not use them for extended parking.
4. Operational notes
Use of broken‑boundary streets. Lonsdale Street, Collins Street and other broken‑boundary streets are not patrolled, but they provide convenient paths to move between segments of the patrol. Only enforce obvious offences such as stopping in clearways or bus lanes when walking along these streets.
Documentation and field verification. The patrol zone definitions and parking‑restriction notes are based on user‑supplied maps and City of Melbourne sources. Some lane names or extents (e.g., Rosslyn Street dead‑end) may require field verification. Officers should note any discrepancies and report them for map updates.
High‑productivity focus. To improve productivity, prioritise checks at known high‑turnover areas (loading zones on William Street, Rosslyn Street and King Street; accessible bays; car‑share bay), and time patrols to coincide with clearway periods on Lonsdale Street. Avoid prolonged patrols on broken boundaries or low‑risk residential bays unless there are reported issues.
