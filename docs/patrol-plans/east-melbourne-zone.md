East Melbourne Patrol Plan for Clarendon–Lansdowne–Victoria Parade Area
Overview

The patrol area depicted in the supplied maps covers the East Melbourne grid between Wellington Parade (west), Hoddle Street (east), Lansdowne/Nicholson/Spring Streets (north) and Clarendon Street/Simpson Street/Wellington Parade South (south). The highlighted streets (yellow segments) represent roads within City of Melbourne’s jurisdiction where parking restrictions apply. The objective is to maximise infringement capture by focusing on time‑limited bays, permit‑only zones and “red sign” restrictions such as loading zones and no‑stopping areas. City of Melbourne’s open‑data parking‑zone API was used to retrieve the official restriction codes and times for each parking zone.

The patrol area (yellow lines) includes segments of Lansdowne St, Albert St, Clarendon St, Powlett St, Grey St, Simpson St, Wellington Parade and Spring/Nicholson St. Brown shading marks the boundary; areas east of Powlett/George/Hotham Streets are outside City of Melbourne’s parking data.

Enforcement strategy for high capture rate
Target permit‑only bays:
– Lansdowne St (Zone 7262) and Albert St near Clarendon (Zone 7235) have PP restrictions. Patrol early (after 08:00) and again mid‑afternoon to catch commuters parking without permits. Verify Area 1a resident permits; issue infringements for missing permits.
– On Victoria Parade (Zone 7287), enforce permit status near Freemasons’ Hospital.
Maximise turnover in time‑limited bays:
– 1P zones (Albert St zone 7246, Clarendon St zone 7243, parts of Lansdowne zone 7260) require hourly sweeps. Record arrival times and re‑check after 1 hour.
– 2P/4P zones: schedule patrol cycles at 2‑hour intervals (9 AM, 11 AM, 1 PM, 3 PM) to capture overstays. Use number‑plate recognition to detect vehicles that remain beyond the allowed period.
– Metered zones (MP2P/MP4P) on Spring St and Wellington Parade demand checking both time limits and payment compliance via ticket or PayStay app. Non‑payment can be fined immediately.
– Mixed restrictions (Lansdowne St zone 7260) require careful reading of signage: 1P applies during the morning; 2P after 09:30. Adjust enforcement accordingly.
Exploit “red sign” zones:
– Loading Zone (LZ30) on Spring St (zone 7274) operates 09:00‑16:00 Mon‑Fri. Only vehicles actively loading or unloading goods may stop for up to 30 minutes. Issue fines to passenger vehicles and vehicles exceeding 30 minutes.
– No‑stopping areas on Wellington Parade South (Yarra Park), around the MCG and coach bays, are not in the dataset but are clearly signed. Stopping is prohibited; a single observation is sufficient to issue a notice.
– Clearway times on Victoria Parade and Wellington Parade (outside of the dataset) still apply. Look for signs indicating AM/PM clearways (e.g., 7:00‑9:00 AM or 4:00‑6:00 PM). Stopping is not allowed during these times.
Adjust patrol timing:
– Start patrol at 07:30 when most restrictions begin; note that some paid-parking and mixed zones start at 09:00 or 09:30.
– Include Saturday mornings (07:30‑12:30) for 2P and 4P zones; many commuters are unaware of weekend restrictions.
– Evening patrols on Spring St (zones 7273–7275) should extend until 22:00 to enforce meter‑parking compliance.
General rules for high capture:
– Permit zones (PP): Only vehicles displaying the specified residential permit may park; all others should be fined.
– Loading zones (LZ30): Restricted to vehicles loading/unloading goods; maximum stay is 30 minutes and only during the signed times.
– Paid parking (MP2P/MP4P): Drivers must purchase a ticket via meter or PayStay and adhere to the time limit.
– Time‑limited parking (1P/2P/4P): Motorists must not exceed the stated duration or relocate within the same parking zone to reset the time. Enforcement officers should use a chalk mark or digital sensor to record arrival time.
Summary

Using the City of Melbourne parking‑zone datasets, the above plan identifies the specific restriction codes, days and times for each highlighted street within the patrol zone. By concentrating efforts on permit‑only segments, short‑stay bays and the loading zone on Spring Street, officers can maximise infringement capture. Regular patrol cycles (hourly for 1P zones, every two hours for 2P/4P zones) and thorough checking of PayStay payments will help ensure high compliance and efficient enforcement.
