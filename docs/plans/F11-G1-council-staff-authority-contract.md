## Scope and invariants

This contract defines a proposed council-owned staff lifecycle and authority boundary. Council ownership is the `municipality_id`; every operational grant is bound to one currently owned operating context. The existing immutable `council_principal_owners` and `sessions.municipality_id` controls (migrations 014–015) are retained. They establish principal ownership and grant/context ownership only; they do not establish authority to create staff, approve grants, or recover an account.

Council staff administration remains separate from operational CommandMAPS and Platform operator authority. The existing `users.role` operational values (`DISPATCHER`, `OFFICER`, `SUPERVISOR`) and the legacy bootstrap `ADMIN` are not staff-administration permissions. `ADMIN` receives no operational request, personnel, dispatch, or generic records access. F07 D02 remains a one-time designated first-administrator activation flow; it grants no general staff-management, recovery, or reactivation authority. F08 D03 retained-review, write serialization, officer restrictions, and no-grant-expansion-on-reenable rules remain in force. F09 suspension remains separate.

Each council independently owns identities, sessions, employment/access status, grants, approvals, work, and records. A matching person, email, credential, or upstream SSO session never links councils, exposes cross-council discovery, or confers another council's authority. A shared authentication provider is compatible only if it creates separately audience-bound council principals and sessions and cannot silently mint another council session.

Q01–Q05/Q07 and the owner's direct answers establish the decision rules below. Routine same-context branch actions do not require an operator. Every planned high-impact change to council authority requires exact independent council authorization and privileged CivicMAPS operator participation under Q07. The proposed G1 workflow routes such changes through a privileged operator for execution and a separate second operator for approval; no implementation may make these controls conditional on whether a particular endpoint happens to be hosted by Platform. A separately assured council-managed alternative requires a future contract amendment and audit. Actors are distinct natural persons, not merely separate accounts. Unclassified or out-of-scope operations are denied.

A change that cannot be placed in this table is denied pending contract amendment and independent reassessment. `OFFICER`↔`DISPATCHER` changes in one approved context are classified as routine only because Q01 assigns routine branch-scoped access approvals and the owner specifically reserved `SUPERVISOR` to central security. Any increase outside those named role/context bounds is elevated. The subject never approves its own increase, including a branch-scoped role change.

Each branch delegation is a council-signed record containing the exact branch/context IDs, enumerated allowed actions, permitted operational roles (`DISPATCHER`, `OFFICER`), effective time range, council-approved expiry, delegation version, delegator, independent verifier, qualification evidence reference and revocation state. The council must document that the branch has the governance, compliance or risk function required by Q01 and approve its qualification criteria; a separate council verifier records that the named delegate meets those criteria. One level only; no child delegation. Missing, expired, revoked, unqualified or stale delegation fails closed. Central security resumes the work when a branch lacks that capability.

## Authentication, session, and delegation rules

Every council administrator must use MFA, consistent with Q04. A council session is created only after council-specific identity binding and current membership verification. Operational sessions do not imply staff-management authority. In particular, legacy `users.role` and global email identity never authorize a staff endpoint or widen access across councils. Staff-administration routes resolve from the council-scoped principal, active membership, current staff-authority record, and exact council/branch scope; they do not require an operational user or operating-context grant. Operational requests additionally require an active operating-context binding and that principal's exact current context grant; a staff-administration role cannot substitute for it. Any migration must retire or explicitly map legacy global-role checks before enabling staff provisioning; unknown or ambiguous records fail closed.

The preferred path is suitable council SSO; the signed choice permits a separately governed CivicMAPS MFA fallback. No provider is selected or purchased. The council's login audience, callback, issuer/subject binding, and session namespace are council-specific. A successful login to one council cannot automatically authenticate to another. Session records carry an immutable council owner and explicit context; on disable/revocation they are invalidated in the same transaction or checked against a current revocation version on every request. Session renewal cannot restore a revoked grant.

Delegations are durable, attributable authority records and never derive from the delegate's operational role. Every delegated permission names allowed action classes, exact council contexts, start/end, delegator, and policy version. The proposed routine action set is limited to inviting, changing, and revoking `DISPATCHER`/`OFFICER` access in that exact context; it excludes `SUPERVISOR`, cross-branch or structural changes, council verification, invitation redemption by anyone other than the subject, and any delegation changes. Expiry and revocation remove effective authority immediately; no action or approval may outlive or exceed the delegation. **Owner confirmed in this session (22 October 2026):** one delegation level only, exact scope, no sub-delegation, and expiry approved by the council. The exact routine action set is a proposed interpretation of the owner's direction and is submitted for independent G1 review; do not implement it until the contract passes.

Identity proofing and administrator/custodian independence require evidence from a council-designated verifier distinct from the subject and from the requested approver/executor. Record verifier identity, method identifier, evidence reference, timestamp, and policy version; do not copy sensitive proof artifacts into general audit. Do not permit a Platform operator to self-appoint or unilaterally certify council authority. **Owner confirmed in this session (22 October 2026):** each council designates an independent verifier and configures its own proofing method.

The owner also confirmed in this session (22 October 2026) that general staff invitations use a 24-hour, one-use hashed token, consistent with D02. The signed policy requires two distinct active, MFA-protected administrators and a separate independent recovery custodian. Before a council enables staff administration, an authorized council decision must appoint both administrators, the custodian, and the independent verifier; the verifier records identity-proofing evidence using that council’s configured method. Initial appointment and replacement each require separately attributable verification and the applicable independent council approval. The custodian is the sole council-side recovery authorizer; administrator quorum is not an additional recovery gate. Custodian loss pauses recovery until a replacement custodian is appointed through the ordinary independently approved appointment path. A disable or revoke is permitted without satisfying the two-admin quorum. No one person may both authorize and execute recovery, even through separate credentials. The exact customer-side appointment authority is council-specific and must be recorded before enabling that council; this contract does not identify real people or authorize production enrollment.

### Initial authority commissioning — restricted setup-only proposal (unapproved)

The 24 October receipt answers only how the product owner says their own identity was proofed through a government service and which MFA method they approve for their own authentication. It does not appoint the owner as a council actor or establish who can authorize a council appointment.

To avoid circular dependence on an approver who cannot yet exist in the application, the following restricted setup-only sequence is proposed for owner decision and independent review. Before application activation, a council decision-maker identified through an externally admitted council trust record would submit an exact commissioning proposal naming the council, first central-security target, two administrator nominees, recovery custodian nominee, independent verifier, and appointment payload/version. The record source and admission method must be established outside the uncommissioned application and retained as provenance; this contract does not define or presume that source, who admits it, or who has legal appointment authority. A setup-only commissioning service would accept only such externally admitted council records, verify their binding to the named council and payload, and permit no operational session, staff administration, or grant use during setup.

The proposed setup sequence is: (1) an external council authority creates a signed, council-specific commissioning record under a trust root admitted before application activation; the record names the independent council approver, initial central-security target, two administrator nominees, recovery custodian, verifier, exact payload/version, issue/expiry times, unique nonce, and policy/proofing references; it carries provenance showing that the external authority admitted the named approver and the approver's authority for this exact decision; (2) after separately proofing that approver's identity and authority, the admitted external authority issues a one-use setup credential scoped to this council ID, record ID, exact payload hash/version, nonce, and expiry. The named approver uses it to explicitly sign or strongly authenticate approval of those exact values. The credential cannot authorize another record/payload, establish an ordinary staff session, or exercise continuing council permission. The approver must be a distinct person from the commissioning requester, every appointment subject, and both privileged operators; (3) a setup-only verifier checks the record signature and trust chain, council binding, approver admission and proofing, scoped credential and signed approval, role/person separation, validity window, revocation state, payload hash, and nonce, without creating an ordinary staff session; (4) one privileged operator executes and a distinct second privileged operator approves the same payload hash under Q07; (5) immediately before one transaction records the accepted record, exact council approval and Q07 actions, initial appointments as pending, and audit receipt, all credentials, approvals, versions, expiry, revocation state, role separation, and nonce are rechecked under a uniqueness lock on council and nonce; and (6) each nominee separately completes the council-configured identity-proofing and MFA/activation checks. Immediately before activation the system rechecks the same trust record, approver proof and exact approval, Q07 actions, payload/version, expiry, revocation state, nonce, and nominee checks. Staff administration activates only when a verification inventory confirms the external trust chain and approver admission, exact council decision and payload hash/version, all distinct required roles, each identity/qualification result, both operator actions, current record validity, and immutable audit receipt. A payload change invalidates the approval and requires a new admitted record and approval. The inventory itself is versioned and attributable; missing, ambiguous, expired, revoked, replayed, or mismatched evidence keeps all appointments pending and the council disabled.

The proposed setup endpoint accepts no ordinary staff session and no caller-selected authority fields; it accepts only the signed external record, the approver's separate record/payload-scoped proof and explicit approval, and an idempotency key. A retry with the same council, nonce, payload hash, approval, and key returns the original non-secret receipt; a changed payload/key/approval conflicts, and concurrent submissions serialize on the council commissioning slot. Before pending creation and again before activation, a verified external-record or approval revocation, expired/invalid proof, identity-role collision, or failed nominee proofing cancels or holds the pending commissioning state, invalidates its setup nonce, retains audit/history, and requires a newly admitted record and approval; partial roles never become usable. The expiry duration, approved trust-root/admitter, authority to issue the first council decision, and approved approver authentication method remain owner decisions. This is an unapproved proposal: the 24 October receipt establishes none of these. Do not implement or enable it until those decisions and independent review are complete. D02 first-admin activation remains insufficient and is not reused as an authority grant.

## Proposed API contract (not implemented)

All council endpoints require a council-audience authenticated session, CSRF protection for mutations, current context/authority evaluation, request limits, and structured audit, except invitation activation, which uses the single-use invitation secret and configured independent proofing flow as a narrowly scoped authentication bootstrap. Activation accepts no caller-selected council, principal, role, or context; it only redeems the invitation’s immutable payload. It creates the council-scoped principal after proofing and atomically binds that principal to the pending membership and authorized grants. Platform operator endpoints remain separate and can execute only approved operations.

Errors contain no secret or cross-council existence details. External identity-provider failures do not create partial membership or grant state.

## Additive schema and migration proposal (not implemented)
