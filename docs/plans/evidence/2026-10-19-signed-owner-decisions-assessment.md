## Reconciliation with approved behavior and current implementation

No material contradiction requiring the owner to repeat a selection was found. The signed conditions govern hybrid interpretations. These are prospective design directions, not descriptions of shipped behavior.

- **D02 remains the limit for the current bootstrap.** [Approved D02](../F07-D02-decisions.md) authorizes one designated first-admin synthetic bootstrap, pending-token reissue and disable—not general staff management, reactivation or recovery. Preserve exact council/context binding, single-use hashed tokens with 24-hour expiry, no plaintext token replay, and disable revocation of sessions/outstanding tokens. Do not give bootstrap credentials recovery, staff-management or record access. The signed two-admin/custodian model needs its own enrollment/replacement/migration design. Preserve Alpha's usable admin and Beta's disabled admin/no sessions.
- **Q6 adds a new actor boundary; it does not change D03.** [Approved D03](../F08-D03-decisions.md) controls the commercial ceiling/catalogue, D03 retained review after disable, officer restrictions, write/disable ordering and audited new-version re-enable. Existing pack-change routes remain operator-controlled; council activation within the ceiling is not implemented. Design separate ceiling and activation state/version domains and prevent ceiling restoration from silently reactivating a feature or revoked grant.
- **Checkpoint B remains current-state evidence, not architecture approval.** The [authority/data-flow matrix](2026-10-19-authority-data-flow-matrix.md) documents no council staff lifecycle or council activation API/UI; an operationally excluded bootstrap `ADMIN`; runtime `civicmaps_f06_runtime` with `BYPASSRLS` and broad control-table access; and provider/deployment/support powers beyond app-role grants. Those are observed development capabilities, not evidence of misuse or proof of production isolation. Q2/Q8 do not cure them.
- Q5's exact, council-approved operator execution and Q7's independent second-operator check are distinct controls. Neither allows operator discretion to set council grants nor requires operator involvement in routine branch work. Q7 does not impose operator approval on routine Q6 activation. Q9 allows a custodian design but does not add the expressly unselected dual-approval rule for every export.

## Advanced reassessment and refinements

The independent reassessment is published as [the companion review](2026-10-19-signed-owner-decisions-review.md).
