## Findings and clarifications

1. **Q02: preserve full council independence.** Each council independently administers identity, sessions, employment/access status, grants, work and records; prohibit cross-council sharing, inheritance, visibility and operational federation. Do not let a shared person record, email match or SSO account become a shared lifecycle/authorization principal. “Identity matching across councils” must mean only preventing accidental account linking and proving independent administration; it grants no cross-council discovery/linking permission. Specify council-specific authentication audiences and session issue/invalidation, including whether one upstream IdP session could silently create another council session. Shared authentication technology is allowed only if it preserves these boundaries.
2. **Q04: two administrators plus a distinct custodian.** This deliberately modifies option C's one-admin wording. Specify custodian independence, enrollment/verification, replacement and loss cases. A disable that leaves fewer than two active admins must still take effect promptly and must not silently authorize or reactivate a replacement. The two admins do not gain recovery approval power. Define how Q07 second-operator approval applies when recovery is high impact.
3. **Q05/Q07: two distinct approvals.** Independent council approval authorizes the exact high-impact change; Q07 separately requires independent second-person operator approval for high-impact operator action. A council approver cannot substitute for the second operator. Planned changes to council authority need both. Define exact change/version binding, approver independence, expiry, stale target/version, intervening revocation, replay and failure handling. Do not infer operator discretion over council grants or universal operator involvement in routine branch work.
4. **Q06: new activation permission.** Existing pack-change routes do not implement council activation. Define separate commercial ceiling and council activation state/version domains and effective capability, including ceiling reduction, stale activation, concurrent writes and later ceiling restoration. A ceiling change must not silently restore activation or revoked grants. Routine council activation does not require Q07 operator approval and cannot be inferred from `ADMIN`.
5. **Q08: assessment target only.** B+A calls for distinct council runtime, infrastructure/data boundaries and credentials, with controlled provider support/deployment authority. It is not merely shared-database row isolation and is not a promise of provider plaintext non-access. Topology/cost assessment may proceed; production resources, procurement, spend, live access or deployment do not follow. Existing broad broker/runtime powers remain current-state findings, not cured by this decision.
6. **Q09: do not add unselected export dual approval.** Design a scoped records custodian and controlled export, without option B's dual approval for each exceptional export. Define appointment, purpose, record set, recipient, audit and departure/closure/exit. Specify retained access to records whose original grants/context no longer apply without rewriting origins or relaxing unrelated operational role restrictions.
7. **Q10: human authorization for every disposal batch.** B controls; A contributes schedule/hold verification and staged assessment, not its option of future autonomous disposal. Software may identify candidates; an execution service may perform only an exact approved batch and cannot expand it. Bind approval to a manifest/version, recheck holds/dependencies, record partial outcomes/audit, and address backup/secondary copies. No immediate disposal, universal retention period or indefinite retention without review. Customer schedule/hold decisions gate only affected behavior.
8. **Q11: preserve evidence duties.** Preserve forensics, provenance, holds and audit; independently review incidents and validate revocations/grants/isolation before restoration. Conditional independent specialists are triggered by defined severity/capability/compromise/exposure/independence/recovery needs, not every incident. Councils retain statutory, service, privacy and notification decisions; no universal notification deadline is established.
9. **Q12: external assessment is required within scope.** A qualified independent assessor is required for initial production release and material security/architecture changes, subject to separate procurement/budget approval. Budget nonapproval does not waive it; the affected release/change remains unapproved. Define materiality, assessor independence/competence, evidence scope and timing. Do not weaken material security/architecture changes to architecture alone or extend assessment to every routine correction. The assessor cannot replace operator sign-off or each council's acceptance. This review does not satisfy that procurement-dependent assessor requirement.
