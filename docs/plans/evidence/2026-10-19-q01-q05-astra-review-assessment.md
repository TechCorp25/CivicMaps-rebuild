## Findings and required response

Astra found the owner choices substantially represented but the contract not sufficient for broad authority implementation. Its decisive source finding is that synthetic `shared@alpha.example` (`00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000006`) has an explicit Beta grant through Beta municipality context `10000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000003`; the existing test intentionally allows that same principal into both Alpha and Beta contexts and adds an Alpha officer's Beta grant. This repository fixture conflicts with signed Q02. It does not prove a live shared principal exists and is distinct from the actual Alpha usable / Beta disabled-with-no-sessions first-admin state.

Astra also found global unique email lookup, user-level role, and sessions without immutable council binding; a council ownership change cannot be implemented by a session field alone. D02 disable sets the Beta admin inactive and removes sessions/tokens but does not tombstone the existing context grant row. Its owner is Beta; inactive status makes that legacy grant non-effective. The revised contract therefore does not falsely claim the row is revoked.

Further design gaps concerned council-level administration scope versus operational context grants; precise actor rights and approval separation; recovery/disable/rehire transitions; atomic approval/execution and current-state revalidation; the D03 retained-review exception at the Q06 boundary; and restoration of a pre-revocation backup. The review also requires distinguishing synthetic fixture manifest from a production ownership algorithm, fail-closed behavior for ambiguous real owners, council-scoped login behavior for duplicate emails, positive same-council and multi-council cases, and residual `BYPASSRLS`/database-owner trust.

## Disposition and revisions recorded

The signed record is not reopened. The first bounded slice is **synthetic principal ownership enforcement** before new council administration: every principal has one explicit immutable council owner; session and route/context access are bound to that council; cross-council grant creation is denied; the known shared Alpha principal keeps its Alpha access and its Beta grant is retired with history; no principal/credential/grant is cloned. Beta's disabled initial admin and Alpha's usable initial admin remain separate preserved constraints. Unclassified owner state fails closed; no ownership is inferred from email, home fields or selected context. The synthetic manifest is never the customer/production migration algorithm.

The revised design adds: council-bound session issuance and authentication, explicit duplicate-email/audience semantics for a later Q03 slice with generic non-disclosing failure, same-council positive and three-or-more-council tests, direct grant validation, exact file/migration/API scope, explicit owner-map preflight, D02 redemption owner assignment, retained Beta grant row handling, approval atomicity/revalidation requirements for later workflows, D03 retained-read exception, fail-closed old backup recovery limits and the residual runtime/database bypass boundary. It keeps user-level operational roles and global unique emails as declared first-slice limitations; it does not claim full Q02/Q03 completion.

The prior shared-service test's cross-council success is explicitly superseded by the signed Q02 direction. Historical requests, audit, receipts, binding IDs and actor IDs are preserved without access inheritance. No Beta replacement principal or grant is created by this migration. A later Beta identity enrollment requires a separate contract and explicit grant.
