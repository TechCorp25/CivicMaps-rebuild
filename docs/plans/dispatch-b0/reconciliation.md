## Design conflict

The design-system [RadioCodeBadge](../../../_ds/components/RadioCodeBadge.jsx) records the owner ruling: code is visible and the paired label expands on interaction, with the label in the collapsed accessible name and `aria-expanded`. [Handover](../../../_ds/HANDOVER.md) repeats that ruling. The older expansion architecture's code-plus-label language and B2 examples should not silently override this later component ruling. Emergency patterns may need visible explanatory text based on their own safety requirements; this decision does not approve an operational emergency or communications service. Any implementation must verify keyboard and screen-reader behaviour in its actual context, and trace exceptions to their authorised context.

### D-GIS decision boundary

The Google Places (New) development direction and protected preview-key reuse are approved in the map override; they do not resolve the authoritative address and zone/reference datasets, their provenance and licence, zone resolution authority, or travel routing provider and travel-mode choices. Provider terms, billing/restriction qualification and disclosure remain separate live-use questions. D3 spatial selection and D4 zone recommendations must not treat browser or unqualified provider results as authoritative eligibility; D5 travel/location claims depend on the applicable approved data and location policy. See [D-GIS](../dispatch-build/decisions-and-dependencies.md) and [M5](../dispatch-build/milestones/M5-shared-spatial-and-travel-routing-context.md).
